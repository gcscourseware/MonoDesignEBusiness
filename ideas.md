# MONO DESIGN AGENCY — Design Direction

## Chosen direction
**The Editorial Marketplace** — a premium creative marketplace that feels like a confident independent design studio: Swiss editorial grids, contemporary digital craft and a quietly technical SaaS layer.

## Design system
- **Design movement:** contemporary editorial minimalism with Swiss grid discipline and restrained digital-product cues.
- **Core principles:** clarity before decoration; designer work is the hero; disciplined monochrome; whitespace as structure; commercial actions are obvious; tactile detail is limited to portfolio imagery and subtle borders.
- **Color philosophy:** near-black ink `#111111`, white `#FFFFFF`, warm paper `#F7F7F5`, cool pale grey `#E7E7E4`, medium graphite `#72726F`; one single restrained citron accent `#D7F36B` for primary actions, focus states and key status cues only. Avoid additional saturated accents.
- **Layout paradigm:** 12-column editorial grid on desktop; stacked cards on mobile; large left-aligned editorial headlines; offset artwork tile compositions; generous margins; simple horizontal rules and clear section numbers.
- **Signature elements:** compact geometric Mono symbol, oversized editorial heading, pill-shaped but minimal category labels, thin rules, black-on-white cards, gallery tiles with small work metadata, small citron action chips and a crisp project timeline.
- **Interaction philosophy:** fast discovery, visible active filters, predictable forms, immediate local response for ordinary actions, confirmed server responses for checkout/role changes; no decorative interactions that obscure the marketplace.
- **Animation:** restrained 160–220 ms opacity/translate transitions, reduced-motion support, no animated marquee or distracting parallax.
- **Typography system:** an expressive contemporary grotesk for display and a neutral sans for UI/body; use available system sans fallbacks (`Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`) so the interface remains readable without remote font loading. Use tabular numerals for ratings, prices and metrics.
- **Brand essence:** considered taste, professional access, creative confidence and practical collaboration.
- **Brand voice:** direct, warm, concise, helpful and assured; avoid agency jargon.
- **Wordmark/logo:** typeset “MONO” in bold uppercase next to the restrained “DESIGN AGENCY” descriptor. Geometric mark is a bold, memorable M formed from a few broad filled shapes with intentional negative space; do not rely on generated typography.
- **Signature brand color:** citron is an action/status signal only, not a background palette.
